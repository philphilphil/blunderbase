Recommendation

Build the proposal: concept 2 (above and below) with concept 1's ⋯ menu. The four toggles ride the top player row and the ply and score ride the bottom one — both rows exist already, are two-thirds empty and cost the board nothing — so the row under the board is only actions on the left and the transport on the right. The rare studio extras (Back to explorer, the correspondence tree, Copy PGN) go behind ⋯, and the things that exist only in a state (Back to game, Show the engine, Add to library, a running run) stay as tinted chips in that state. Every key is unchanged and every frequent control keeps its word.

Why not the bolder ones: 4 gives the biggest board (about 2.25 rem) but takes the transport out of the board column and cannot wrap the bottom player row, so it needs ⋯ as a safety net and a habit change; worth revisiting if the row is still the limiting height on the owner's own window. 5 (modes) is the clearest screen but the largest change and makes a modeless page modal. 3 and 6 hide frequent controls behind glyphs or a menu. On the phone the proposal is today's two rows with icon toggles and ⋯, which is what makes the worst case fit the two lines the phone's board budget assumes.
